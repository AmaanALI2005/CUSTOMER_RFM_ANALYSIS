RFM Analysis Dashboard | Power BI
An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing customer purchasing behaviour using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis. The project explores customer value, revenue trends, product performance, and customer segments to support data-driven business decisions.

Project Overview
RFM analysis is a customer segmentation method based on three dimensions:
- Recency: How recently a customer made a purchase.
- Frequency: How often a customer makes purchases.
- Monetary: How much a customer spends.
The dashboard brings these measures together with business-level KPIs and interactive filters.

Dashboard Highlights
- Total customers
- Total revenue
- Average customer value
- Revenue trend by year
- Monthly customer trend
- Country-level analysis
- Product revenue analysis
- RFM customer segments
- Interactive slicers for month, quarter, year, country, and segment

Business Value
RFM analysis can help a business:
- Identify high-value and loyal customers.
- Find customers who may be at risk of becoming inactive.
- Prioritize customer retention campaigns.
- Understand purchasing patterns and customer value.
- Support more targeted marketing decisions.

How to Use
1. Download the .pbix file from this repository.
2. Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Explore the report pages and interact with the slicers.
4. Select a month, quarter, year, country, or customer segment to explore the data.
